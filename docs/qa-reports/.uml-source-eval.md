# UML Source Eval — MERMAID vs PUML vs Codebase

> 2026-05-25 · Truth: `app/Models/`, `Controllers/`, `Enums/`, `routes/web.php`

## Class Diagram

**Winner: `UML_FINAL/01_class_diagram_as_built.puml`** ✅

- 9 `OrderStatus` + 5 `PaymentStatus` (incl `refunded`) match enum. Mermaid bahasa Indonesia ringkas, gak ada `refunded`.
- `RentalUnit` 4 case + `UserRole` 4 role match. Mermaid abstract tanpa enum.
- Polymorphic `Payment.orderable` (morphMany) dijelaskan + note. Mermaid cuma `*--` tanpa keterangan polymorphic.
- AuditLog + Spatie HasRoles akurat di PUML.

Patch PUML: tag `{missing — gap}` di `Driver.rentalOrders` & `VehicleCategory.overtimePenalty` **sudah eksis** di model, hapus. Tambah kolom `refunded_at`, `refunded_by`, `refund_reason` di Payment.

## Use Case

**Winner: `02_use_case_diagram_as_built.puml`** ✅ (telak)
